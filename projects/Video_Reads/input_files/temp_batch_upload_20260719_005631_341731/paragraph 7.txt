Once both are running, the last step is verification. Open a browser to http://127.0.0.1:8338 — that's Mal trail's default reporting port — and log in with the default credentials, admin and changeme!. If the dashboard loads, the baseline is working. To confirm it's actually seeing traffic, generate something Mal trail will flag — a simple DNS lookup against a known-bad domain will do it — and check that the event shows up in the log.
