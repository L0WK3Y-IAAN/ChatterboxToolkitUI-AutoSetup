8337 is just the default, though — nothing stops you from changing it. Since both sides need to agree on the number, this means editing two different settings that happen to share a value. On the server, that's UDP_PORT in maltrail.conf, the port it listens on. On the sensor, it's the port number baked into that same LOG_SERVER line you set earlier, which is where it sends its reports. Stop both processes, update UDP_PORT on the server and the port in LOG_SERVER on the sensor to match, then start both back up.
